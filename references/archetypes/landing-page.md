# Landing Page

Single-offer page. One offer, one audience, one primary action. Not a homepage.

Reach for `product-proof-saas.md` instead when the mechanism needs a live demo to be believed. Reach for `pricing-page.md` when the decision is which plan.

Gather first: the one primary action, the offer, the ICP and main pain, top 3 objections, traffic source, proof on hand, risk reversal available.

Layout, pick one: A classic hero + sections (a screenshot explains it), B long-form story (skeptics to educate), C minimal conversion (high-intent email traffic, short offer), D comparison (search intent is "X vs Y").

## Section order

1. Headline: outcome plus audience.
2. Subheadline: how, with specificity.
3. Primary CTA: verb plus what they get.
4. One proof signal: logo strip, stat, or short testimonial.
5. Hero visual: product screenshot or video, or one strong illustration.
6. Problem to solution, one section.
7. Benefits, outcome-driven.
8. How it works.
9. Social proof: testimonials or a case study.
10. FAQ.
11. Risk reversal.
12. Final CTA, same as the top.

## Numbers

| Number | Default | Failure prevented |
|---|---|---|
| Primary CTAs above the fold | 1 | Competing actions |
| Proof signals above the fold | 1 | Proof buried at the bottom |
| Subheadline | 1-2 sentences | Paragraph hero |
| Benefits | 3-5 | Feature dump with no outcomes |
| How-it-works steps | 3 | Exhaustive manual |
| FAQ | 6-12 Q/A | Objections left unhandled |
| Risk reversals | At least 1: trial, free plan, no card, cancel anytime, guarantee | Nothing to lower the stakes |
| Responsive range | 390px to 1440px | Mobile layout breaking readability |
| Restrained interactions | 1 | Motion with no job |

## Signature moves

- Ad traffic: mirror the ad headline and visual tone in the hero.
- Specificity over verbs: "Cut weekly reporting from 4 hours to 15 minutes", never "streamline".
- Place proof next to the claim it supports.
- Benefit bullet format: bold benefit, then the proof or detail.
- Headline formulas: "{Outcome} without {pain}", "The {category} for {audience}", "Ship {result} in {time}".

## Do not

- Stack multiple CTAs above the fold.
- Write vague value props ("optimize", "streamline").
- List features with no outcome attached.
- Use "Learn more" or "Submit" as a CTA.
- Index ad-only or time-bound pages; use `noindex`.

## Effects it may use

- `staggered-word-reveal` on the headline.
- `masked-reveal` on the hero visual.
