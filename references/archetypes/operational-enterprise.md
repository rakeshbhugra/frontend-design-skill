# Operational Enterprise

Enterprise AI, automation, security, or ops page that shows what the system does, where it stops, who approves, and how failures roll back.

Reach for `product-proof-saas.md` instead when the buyer is self-serve and the demo is the pitch. Reach for `pricing-page.md` when the decision is which plan.

## Section order

1. Header: product, solutions, security, case studies, one qualified action.
2. Hero (dark): boundary-led headline left. Right: one labeled sample workflow trace with intake, policy, prepared action, audit packet, human approval gate, checks recorded, pending approval, rollback target.
3. Metrics (white interlude): verified numbers only, each with scope, source, timeframe.
4. Solution rows: expandable, each exposing summary, permissions, action, approval, output, audit, exception, rollback.
5. Product demo: real or labeled sample data, deterministic state changes.
6. Security: controls tied to concrete risks, no unsupported badges.
7. Case studies: verified implementation facts kept separate from marketing interpretation.
8. Testimonials: grayscale portraits, only when licensed and real.
9. FAQ: ownership, data handling, integrations, review, failure, procurement.
10. Final CTA: who the product is for and what happens after submission.

## Numbers

| Number | Default | Failure prevented |
|---|---|---|
| Spectral treatments | 1, restrained | Neon cyber gradients |
| Radius | Low, square media, minimal shadow | Consumer-app softness on an ops product |
| Fields per solution row | 8 | Capability claims with no control model |
| States designed per row | 8: loading, unavailable integration, insufficient permission, stale data, denied approval, partial completion, rollback, error | Demo that only shows the happy path |
| Control feedback | 160-220ms | Sluggish disclosures |
| Section entrance | 500-760ms | Reveals that feel snappy instead of deliberate |
| Parallax | Below 5% | Atmosphere ahead of data legibility |
| Chapter handoff | Hard black to white | Gratuitous smooth scrolling |

## Signature moves

- Near-black, warm white, muted gray. White chapters reserved for operational explanation and metric pauses.
- High-x-height sans with compact mono labels and tabular numerals.
- Hairline grid lines as the only ornament.
- Solution rows open with Enter or Space, hover and focus cues equivalent, text equivalent for every diagram.
- Case study links carry the buyer into a demo or waitlist with non-sensitive intent prefilled, with consent.

## Do not

- Glowing AI orbs, particle clouds, cyberpunk decoration.
- Invent metrics, customers, badges, compliance, availability, or performance claims.
- Promise automation with no permissions or exception model.
- Leap from a feature list straight to a contact form.
- Bury risk, audit, failure, or rollback in vague copy.
